## Basis and maintenance

The documents reflect the prototype’s local storage, optional Family Controls authorization, App Group extensions, local notifications, manual history export, and absence of app accounts, analytics SDKs, ads, or purchases. The Screen Time timing limitations are disclosed rather than described as a verified hard wall-clock lock.

Reviewed against Apple’s [privacy review requirements](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/review/guidelines/#privacy), [Standard EULA](https://www.apple.com/legal/internet-services/itunes/dev/stdeula/), and GitHub’s [Pages hosting disclosure](https://docs.github.com/en/pages/getting-started-with-github-pages/what-is-github-pages). These documents describe this product; they are not a guarantee of compliance in every jurisdiction or a claim of lawyer review.

Update these documents before changing data practices, adding paid features, changing the operator, or changing the intended age group or launching for schools. Confirm the shipped build matches the disclosures before using its privacy label or submitting to the App Store.

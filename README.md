# Reverloop legal documents

Privacy and product terms for Reverloop, a focus app where accumulated study time grows a personal terrarium.

- [Privacy policy](PRIVACY.md)
- [Terms of use](TERMS.md)
- [Support](SUPPORT.md)

## Draft status

Prepared September 24, 2026 against the current native iPhone research prototype. Operator identity and a private support/privacy email are awaiting confirmation. The intended age group, launch territories, and release effective date also need to be set. These documents should not be represented as finalized release policies or lawyer-reviewed terms.

The app source is separate; this repository contains only legal and support documents. No private study data, signing information, or credentials are included.

## Basis and maintenance

The documents reflect the prototype’s local storage, optional Family Controls authorization, App Group extensions, local notifications, manual history export, and absence of app accounts, analytics SDKs, ads, or purchases. The Screen Time timing limitations are disclosed rather than described as a verified hard wall-clock lock.

Reviewed against Apple’s [privacy review requirements](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/review/guidelines/#privacy), [Standard EULA](https://www.apple.com/legal/internet-services/itunes/dev/stdeula/), and GitHub’s [Pages hosting disclosure](https://docs.github.com/en/pages/getting-started-with-github-pages/what-is-github-pages). This is a product-specific draft, not a guarantee of compliance in every jurisdiction.

Update these documents before changing data practices, adding paid features, changing the operator, or launching for children or schools. Confirm the shipped build matches the disclosures before using its privacy label or submitting to the App Store.
